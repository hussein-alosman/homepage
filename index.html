01 / PROFILE

About

Hussein Al Osman is a Professor at the University of Ottawa and Vice-Dean, Student Affairs in the Faculty of Engineering. He leads the Multimedia Processing and Interaction Group.

He earned his B.A.Sc., M.A.Sc., and Ph.D. at the University of Ottawa. His work connects affective computing, multimedia systems, human–computer interaction, and biomedical engineering, with particular interest in multimodal emotion recognition and remote physiological signal measurement.

His research has also explored serious games for rehabilitation and education and has led to peer-reviewed publications, patents, and technology transfer.
